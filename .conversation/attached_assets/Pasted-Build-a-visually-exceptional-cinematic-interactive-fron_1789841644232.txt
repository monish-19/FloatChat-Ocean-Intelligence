==================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT
==================================================

FloatChat is a conversational, 4D exploration platform for Indian Ocean ARGO and BGC-Argo float data.

A marine scientist, oceanographer, or policy analyst can ask a natural-language question such as:

"Where is the Arabian Sea warmest right now?"

The system retrieves grounded oceanographic data and presents the answer visually.

Under the question is a three-layer retrieval architecture:

1. Semantic retrieval
   - Resolves fuzzy/concept-based questions.

2. Structured SQL retrieval
   - Handles explicit spatial, temporal, and variable constraints.

3. Grounded LLM fallback
   - Answers open-ended questions ONLY using retrieved evidence.
   - It must say "INSUFFICIENT DATA" instead of inventing scientific values.

A lightweight intent-detection agent recognizes multi-hop questions such as:

"Find floats with a similar anomaly pattern near this region."

These questions can be routed through a Neo4j knowledge graph.

The final experience combines:
- Conversational AI
- ARGO/BGC-ARGO ocean data
- 4D spatial exploration
- 3D visualization
- Real float trajectories
- Depth
- Time
- Oceanographic anomalies
- Knowledge graph reasoning
